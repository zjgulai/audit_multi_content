BACK-TO-WORK Pumping Checklist
A simple one-page plan for
your workday pumping routine
Momcozy × Aeroflow Breastpumps | Melissa Kotlen, RN, IBCLC I
Sarah Van Zandt, IBCLC, PMH-C
1. Before Your First Day Back
Test your pumping setup before the first
workweek.
口
Confirm that pumping feels comfortable
and your flange fit works for you.
Know your pump parts, charger/power
source, and basic setup.
Identify when and where you can
realistically pump during your workday.
Plan around your baby's usual feeding
pattern, your time apart, and your
available work breaks.
Check what workplace pumping
protections and space may apply to you.
3. Have A Backup Plan
Know one backup option if your pump
does not cooperate.
• Consider hand expression or a backup
manual pump.
Keep extra pump parts at work or in
your bag.
L
J
Carry a spare charger or power cord.
If safe milk storage is not available,
keep prioritizing comfort and regular
milk removal.
2. Pack Your Pump-At-Work Bag
• Pump + charger or power source
Correct-fit flanges or inserts
Bottles or milk storage bags
L
Cooler bag + frozen ice packs, if needed
Spare parts for the pieces most likely to
get lost or fail
口
Labels or a simple tracking system
Nursing pads + an extra shirt
Water + snack
4. Keep The Routine Flexible
口
Aim for regular milk removal—not
perfect timing
If baby feeds before you leave, adjust
the timing of your first pump.
If meetings or breaks shift, adjust the
remaining sessions as needed.
A shorter session can still provide some
milk removal and relief when time is
limited.
Avoid making skipped milk removals
your regular pattern.
Keep it simple: the best routine is one you can actually repeat on a real workday.

BACK-TO-WORK Pumping Checklist
A simple one-page plan for
your workdaypumping routine
Momcozy x Aeroflow Breastpumps | Melissa Kotlen, RN, IBCLC |
Sarah Van Zandt, IBCLC, PMH-C
l. Before Your First Day Back
3. Have A Backup Plan
Test your pumping setup before the first
workweek.
O Know one backup option ifyour pump
does not cooperate.
Consider hand expression or a backup
manual pump.
Keep extra pump parts at work or in
your bag.
Carry a spare charger or power cord.
If safe milk storage is not available,
O keep prioritizing comfort and regular
milk removal.
O Confirm that pumping feels comfortable
and your flange fit works foryou.
O Knowyour pump parts, charger/power
source, and basic setup.
O Identify when and where you can
realistically pump during your workday.
O Plan around your baby's usual feeding
pattern, your time apart, and your
available work breaks.
O Check what workplace pumping
protections and space may apply to you.
2. Pack Your Pump-At-Work Bag
4. Keep The Routine Flexible
Pump + charger or power source
O Correct-fit flanges or inserts
O Bottles or milk storage bags
O Cooler bag + frozen ice packs, if needed
O Spare parts for the pieces most likely to
get lost or fail
Aim for regular milk removal-not
perfect timing.
If baby feeds before you leave, adjust
the timing of your first pump.
If meetings or breaks shift, adjust the
remaining sessions as needed.
A shorter session can still provide some
milk removal and relief when time is
limited.
O Labels or a simple tracking system
Nursing pads + an extra shirt
O Water + snack
O Avoid making skipped milk removals
your regular pattern.
Keep it simple: the best routine is oneyou can actually repeat on a real workday.

Learn About Bath Items for Babies
Bathing your child is a wonderful opportunity for
them to relax. The most practical way to bathe your
baby is to use special baby bath essentials. When
your child is older, there are different systems to
install it in the large bathtub: deckchair or bath chair
and bath ring. Let's find out together the essential
bath items for babies.
Bath Essentials for Cozy Bath Time
Towel
For babies, there are many cute
models, some with a small hood. This
will keep your head warm and dry it
well. You can also opt for a bathrobe so
that your child stays warm while you
wait for you to dress them.
Thermometer
It is an inexpensive purchase, but
one that can prevent many
accidents. Indeed, the water must
have a precise temperature, which
must be between 35 and 37
degrees Celsius.
Bathing Chair
With its headrest and suction cups
that hold the deckchair even with
baby's many movements, you will
have your hands free to wash your
child and enjoy these moments of
happiness spent with your baby.
Bathtub
It adapts to your baby's size to facilitate
washing. Be careful, even if it is the
right size for your infant; remember
always to hold his head so that he does
not go underwater!
Choosing the right product can keep your baby
more safe. All you need to choose the right essentials
that make the moment of complicity to the moment
of relaxation and well-being.

here: Bathe A Baby
