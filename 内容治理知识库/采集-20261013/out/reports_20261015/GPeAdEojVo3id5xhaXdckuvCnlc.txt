<b>有库内条款依据</b><br/><b>原因</b>：H05 虚假安全承诺（best/#1/No.1/最佳/最好等排名最高级按2026-09-09口径放开，海外广告允许，不再判险）<br/><b>依据</b>：《措辞表达库 v0（试用版，未发布）》第 W-1.33 monitors」删掉 best，若有真实折扣幅度就写成具体数字，例如「we're offering up to 30% off baby monitors this Black Friday」，拿不到数字就改成「we're offering our Black Friday discounts on baby monitors」；③「Get the best technology at unbeatable prices」整句改成「Get reliable technology at great prices」，同时删掉同句的 unbeatable。另外把「ensure your baby is safe and secure, day or night」改成「help you keep an eye on your baby day or night」，不要用 ensure + safe 这种安全保证句式。（<b>参考了库内反例的建议</b> —— 改法是按素材给出的，<b>不是库规原文</b>）</p><p>2.【产品描述】素材正文 'a smart monitor with Non Wi-Fi and temperature sensors' 与后文 'smart connectivity options'  —— <b>无库内依据 · options' 改为 'Wi-Fi models with app alerts'，让每条卖点都能对上具体型号。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>3.【合规】素材正文第 2 句 'some of our best discounts yet'、第 6 句 'best technology at unbeatable prices'、第 7 句
