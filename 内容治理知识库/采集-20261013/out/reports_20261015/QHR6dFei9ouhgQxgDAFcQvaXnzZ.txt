<title>内容审查报告</title><p>【审查结论：REVIEW】共发现 6 处需人工确认。</p><p>1.【产品描述】正文 「Both our popular Momcozy KleanPal Pro Baby Bottle Washer and Sterilizer and the upgraded Momcozy DeepClean Baby Bottle Washer (D8) automate washing, sterilizing, and drying」  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：“Sterilizer / sterilizing”是绝对灭菌主张——美国市场把灭菌理解为杀灭全部微生物，家用洗烘机若拿不出对应验证数据即为夸大，而且带灭菌宣称的产品在美国按医疗器械口径被审视。<br/><b>依据</b>：U.S. Federal Trade Commission（FTC）《Health Products Compliance Guidance》 · Health Products Compliance Guidance &gt; 4. Mischaracterizations of FDA approval（第 1 段） → <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance">https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：若无灭菌验证数据，把产品名里的“Sterilizer”改为“Sanitizer”、“sterilizing”改为“sanitizing”；若确有数据，保留但补一句限定依据，如“Steam cycle validated to eliminate 99.9% of tested microorganisms”，并在产品页给出测试方法与结果。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>2.【合规】正文 「A professional baby bottle cleaner maintains higher hygiene standards than typical kitchenware.」  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：与“typical kitchenware”作对比并宣称自身“higher hygiene standards”，是无出处的比较级优势主张；“professional”还暗示达到专业/临床级清洁水平。<br/><b>依据</b>：Amazon（Amazon Ads 帮助中心）《Claims and substantiation》 → <a href="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ">https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：删掉比较级与“professional”，改为自述性事实，如“Washes bottles separately from household dishes in a dedicated cycle”；若要保留对比，必须在正文或A+页附上第三方测试机构名称、测试项与结果。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>3.【合规】正文 末句「The First All-in-One Bottle Washer for Newborn Families .」  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：“First”是排他性绝对主张，正文没给认定机构、时间或范围，美国消费者会直接读成“市面上第一款”，一旦不是事实即构成误导；美国站要求这类宣称有实证可查。<br/><b>依据</b>：Amazon（Amazon Ads 帮助中心）《Claims and substantiation》 → <a href="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ">https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：删掉“First”，改为“An All-in-One Bottle Washer for Newborn Families”；若确有依据，补一句把范围收窄的可核查表述，如“The first all-in-one bottle washer launched by Momcozy”，把“第一”限定在自有产品线内。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>4.【措辞表达】正文 首个小标题「Why Your Baby Needs a Dedicated Bottle Cleaner」  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：“Your Baby Needs”把专用清洗器说成婴儿的必需品，暗示不买就有健康风险，属没有实证的必要性/恐惧诉求，婴幼儿健康暗示在美国站是敏感点。<br/><b>依据</b>：U.S. Federal Trade Commission（FTC）《Health Products Compliance Guidance》 · Health Products Compliance Guidance &gt; 4. Mischaracterizations of FDA approval（第 1 段） → <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance">https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：改成“Why Parents Choose a Dedicated Bottle Cleaner”，把主语从“婴儿需要”换成“父母选择”，删掉必要性暗示。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>5.【消费者安全】正文 「…preventing cross-contamination from household dishes.」与「With an infant bottle cleaner ensures a closed, sanitary environment for nipples, baby bottle and pump parts.」  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：“preventing”“ensures”是绝对化的健康获益表述，等于承诺防止交叉污染、保证无菌环境；家用清洗设备若无实测数据支撑，这类承诺会把消费者的卫生安全预期抬到产品达不到的水平。<br/><b>依据</b>：U.S. Federal Trade Commission（FTC）《Health Products Compliance Guidance》 · Health Products Compliance Guidance &gt; Introduction（两项原则之第 2 项） → <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance">https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：“preventing”改为“helping reduce the risk of”，“ensures a closed, sanitary environment”改为“helps create a separate, cleaner space”，并在句末补一行可核查依据，如“Based on internal lab testing of milk protein residue”。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p>
