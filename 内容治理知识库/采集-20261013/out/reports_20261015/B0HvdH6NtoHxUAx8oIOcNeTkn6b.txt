<title>内容审查报告</title><p>【审查结论：BLOCK】共发现 4 处必须修改。</p><p>1.【绝对化与安全承诺】全文，逐字命中 <code>\b(safest|perfect|zero[\s-]?risk|risk[\s-]?free)\b</code>、<code>\bbest\b</code>、<code>\bbest\b</code>  —— <b>有库内条款依据</b><br/><b>原因</b>：H05 虚假安全承诺（best/#1/No.1/最佳/最好等排名最高级按2026-09-09口径放开，海外广告允许，不再判险）<br/><b>依据</b>：《措辞表达库 v0（试用版，未发布）》第 W-1.33 条（链接：https://momcozy-in.feishu.cn/base/Lk3Qb8y2QaVyZns6gprcVNE9nA0?table=tblcbk4XiW6471YN&amp;record=recvuJYdOb1B8v）<br/><b>建议</b>：按三处逐条改：①首句 "Black Friday is the perfect time to snag top-quality baby monitors at incredible prices." 里把 "the perfect time" 换成 "a great time"，整句改成 "Black Friday is a great time to snag top-quality baby monitors at incredible prices."；②"Get the best technology at unbeatable prices and ensure your baby is safe and secure, day or night." 拆成两句改："unbeatable prices" 改成 "low prices"，"ensure your baby is safe and secure" 这个安全保证句式整段删除，替换为功能描述，例如 "With night vision, temperature alerts, and clear audio, you can keep an eye on your baby day or night."；③第二段 "Momcozy Baby Monitors feature high-definition video, clear audio, and smart connectivity options to give you peace of mind wherever you are." 保留原样即可，但全文不得再出现 "ensure/safe and secure" 组合——若后面版本里又写了 "ensure your baby's safety"、"keep your baby 100% safe"、"zero risk"、"risk-free" 这类说法，同样整句删掉，只留可验证的功能点（如 "1080p video"、"Non Wi-Fi connection"、"temperature sensor"）。另外 "some of our best discounts yet" 和 "Get the best technology" 中的 best 属排名类用语可保留，但不要再在前面加 "unbeatable"、"guaranteed" 之类的绝对化修饰。（<b>参考了库内反例的建议</b> —— 改法是按素材给出的，<b>不是库规原文</b>）</p><p>2.【产品描述】素材正文第 2 段第 3 句（"Whether you need ... a smart monitor with Non Wi-Fi and temperature sensors ..."）  —— <b>无库内依据 · 建议转人工</b><br/><b>原因</b>："Non Wi-Fi" 不合英文语法且语义自相矛盾——读者无法判断这台"智能"监视器到底是否需要 Wi-Fi 联网；对关注家庭网络安全的美国家长来说，这个规格点的含糊会直接影响购买判断，也和后文 "smart connectivity options" 冲突。<br/><b>依据</b>：<b>无库内依据</b> —— 知识库条款没覆盖，本条也没给出外部来源（独立语义复核给出的判断，需人工复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：按产品实际情况二选一改写：需要联网写 "a smart Wi-Fi monitor with a temperature sensor"；不需要联网写 "a Wi-Fi-free monitor (no internet connection required) with a temperature sensor"，并把后文 "smart connectivity options" 同步改成对应的连接方式描述。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>3.【合规】素材正文第 4 句（"Get the best technology at unbeatable prices..."），另见第 2 句 "some of our best discounts yet" 与第 1 句 "at incredible prices"  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>："unbeatable prices""incredible prices""some of our best discounts yet" 都是没有比对数据支撑的绝对化价格/品质宣称，美国市场监管与平台都要求这类宣称可被实证，否则构成误导性价格表述。<br/><b>依据</b>：Amazon（Amazon Ads 帮助中心）《Claims and substantiation》 · Claims and substantiation &gt; Prohibited content（全渠道禁止清单） → <a href="https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ">https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G6UU66YLSCUYDULQ</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：把 "unbeatable prices" 换成可核对的具体折扣，例如 "up to 30% off during Black Friday"；把 "incredible prices" 改为 "sale prices"；把 "some of our best discounts yet" 改为带数字与时间范围的 "discounts up to $XX off, [日期]–[日期]"。折扣数字与活动时间需与实际促销一致。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p><p>4.【消费者安全】素材正文第 4 句（"Get the best technology at unbeatable prices and ensure your baby is safe and secure, day or night."）  —— <b>无库内条款 · 依据为外部标准</b><br/><b>原因</b>：婴儿监视器是观察辅助工具，不是安全防护/防猝死设备；文案用 ensure 直接承诺"宝宝安全无忧"，会让美国家长以为买了它就等于有了安全保障，属于对安全效果的绝对化功效承诺，而这类安全功效宣称需要可靠科学实证支撑。<br/><b>依据</b>：U.S. Federal Trade Commission（FTC）《Health Products Compliance Guidance》 · Health Products Compliance Guidance &gt; 科学证据标准（competent and reliable scientific evidence 定义） → <a href="https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance">https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance</a>（登记来源 · 每日复核）<br/><b>建议</b>：删掉 "ensure your baby is safe and secure, day or night"，整句改为观察性、可验证的表述，例如 "Get the best technology at unbeatable prices and keep an eye on your baby day or night"；不要出现 ensure / safe / secure 这类结果性安全承诺词。（<b>无库内依据的改法建议</b> —— 不是库规原文）</p>
